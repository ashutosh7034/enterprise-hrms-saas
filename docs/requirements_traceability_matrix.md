# ENTERPRISE HRMS — REQUIREMENTS TRACEABILITY MATRIX (RTM)

## 1. Document Overview & System Context

This Requirements Traceability Matrix maps all **94 pages** of the source Business Requirements Document (`hr_applications discussion points_1.1.pdf`) to functional requirements, business entities, validation rules, authorization boundaries, and workflows.

### 1.1 Organizational Context & Core Entities
- **Multi-Company Architecture**: The system natively supports two legal entities:
  1. **Vishal Enterprise**
  2. **Vrishal Eng. Pvt. Ltd.**
- **Employment Channels**:
  1. **Direct Employee**: Direct payroll and employment with the company.
  2. **Contractor Employee**: Engaged through third-party contractors/agencies; requires Contractor mapping, contractor billing tracking, and cross-company history.
- **Worker Categories**:
  - Ground Staff (site-based, flexible roster, special weekly-off and holiday calendar rules)
  - Regular Staff (office/plant-based, fixed shifts)
  - Employee Types: Permanent, Temporary, Contract, Trainee, Other
- **Organizational Hierarchy**: Company $\rightarrow$ Location / Site / Branch $\rightarrow$ Department $\rightarrow$ Category $\rightarrow$ Designation.

## 3. Explicit Requirements vs. Analyst Recommendations vs. Unresolved Business Clarifications

### 3.1 Explicit Requirements (Mandatory per PDF)
1. Support exactly two companies: **Vishal Enterprise** and **Vrishal Eng. Pvt. Ltd.**.
2. Strict distinction between **Direct Employees** and **Contractor Employees**.
3. Dedicated **Gate Pass Number** in Employee Master with uniqueness validation and audit trail.
4. Document verification workflow with **eKYC failure resubmission window (2–3 weeks)** and explicit distinction between `eKYC Verified` and `Exceptionally Verified`.
5. Cross-company **Central Blacklist / Whitelist** checking with multi-level approval for rehire restrictions and tokenized/masked Aadhaar support.
6. **Asset Fine Master** defining damages/loss fees recoverable through payroll or direct settlement.
7. **Plant/Site Shutdown Management** supporting Maintenance, Emergency, Planned, and Material Shortage shutdowns with affected employee compensation policies.
8. **Shift Master** supporting overnight/cross-day shifts, grace periods, and half-day/full-day minimum hours.
9. **Holiday Inclusion / Exclusion (Sandwich Rule)** configurable per company/category/leave type.
10. **Notice Period leave policy** enforcing restrictions, approvals, LOP conversions, and notice extensions.
11. **Target OT vs Actual OT** dual-mode overtime calculation.
12. **Statutory Deductions**: Provident Fund (PF), Employee State Insurance (ESI), Professional Tax (PT), and Labour Welfare Fund (LWF).
13. **Full & Final Settlement (F&F)** integrating unpaid wages, leave encashment, notice shortfall, loan balances, and asset fines.
14. **Comp-Off Management** tied directly to verified holiday and weekly-off work punches.
15. **Leave Period Lock** preventing historical alteration post-payroll cutoff without HO Team override.
16. **Multi-Channel Notification Engine** supporting In-App, Email, SMS, and WhatsApp with template management and delivery tracking.
17. **Letter & Form Print Engine** with placeholder substitution, company letterheads, and print audit trail.
18. **Enterprise Reports** covering Muster Roll Form II, PF ECR, ESI returns, bank transfer statements, and automated scheduling.
19. Three strictly segregated role dashboards: **Employee (ESS)**, **Manager (MSS)**, and **HR / HO Admin**.

### 3.2 Analyst Architectural Recommendations (Best Engineering Practices)
1. **Service-Layer Architecture**: Isolate payroll calculation, attendance muster generation, sandwich rule evaluation, and biometric punch pairing into dedicated service classes rather than fat controllers.
2. **Raw Punch Staging Buffer**: Ingest biometric device records into a raw staging table (`biometric_punch_logs`) before batch-processing them into the daily calculated attendance ledger (`attendance_records`).
3. **Database Transactions**: Enforce database transactions across payroll processing, F&F settlement, asset clearance, and multi-level approvals to guarantee ACID consistency.
4. **Queue Worker Architecture**: Offload notification delivery, statutory report generation, and eKYC external API calls to Laravel background queue workers.
5. **Secure Tokenized Aadhaar**: Never store raw 12-digit Aadhaar numbers in plaintext. Store masked display format (`XXXX-XXXX-1234`) and SHA-256 hashed token for duplicate matching.

### 3.3 Unresolved Business Clarifications (Handled via Configurable Settings)
1. **eKYC Gateway Provider**: Configurable driver interface allowing pluggable third-party KYC gateways (Karza/Signzy) or internal manual verification mode.
2. **Biometric Push/Pull Protocol**: Configurable endpoint supporting REST Webhooks, standard ZKTeco ADMS push protocol, and manual CSV punch upload.
3. **Professional Tax (PT) Slabs**: Configurable database table by State and Salary bracket rather than hard-coded values.
4. **Comp-Off Validity Window**: Defaulted to 60 days from accrual date, configurable in Leave Type settings.
5. **Notice Period Extension Ratio**: Defaulted to 1 day extension per 1 day of unapproved leave taken, configurable in Company settings.
